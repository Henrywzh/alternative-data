SOURCES & METHOD

Tokens are OpenRouter's public per-model daily activity, grouped by the model's lab. Estimated spend multiplies prompt and completion tokens by each model's listed price on OpenRouter; discounts, free tiers and caching are not seen, so it is a list-price estimate. ARR annualises a month's estimated spend; the current month is a nowcast from the days observed so far, with a range across the pacing models.

Workloads come from OpenRouter's task classification: the share of tokens or spend in each task over a trailing 30-day window.
