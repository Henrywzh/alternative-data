Freshness and timing note for the Index & ETF monitor: the persisted `close`
pipeline owns completed-session index/ETF history and the artifact carries
`package_info.freshness` for ETF spot, daily technicals, southbound flow and
each configured region/source group. The separate `intraday` CLI/workflow
fetches only a non-persistent Eastmoney spot snapshot for the midday email.
Retrieval-only quotes are `Unverified` because Eastmoney does not provide a
stable exchange observation timestamp; they may be shown as raw context but
are excluded from current entry cost, same-index peer ranking and relative
premium comparisons. Empty spot responses are `Unavailable`. A last-close
reconstruction may remain in the audit row as `quote_basis=last_close`, but it
is never presented as a fresh premium. Persisted premium history is also
session-gated against ETF daily closes at both pipeline and artifact-build
boundaries. Streamlit reads the published US-sector artifact from R2 or local
cache only; it does not trigger a live yfinance fetch during page navigation.

The SHKP research-only model is downstream of the SRPE signal contract rather
than part of the strict financial model. `shkp_indicative_sales_model_monthly`
and `shkp_indicative_sales_model_annual` separate fixed indicative numeric
stakes from gross unquantified-JV activity, with the latter sensitivity-tested
at 25%/50%/75%. The scenario, phase-summary and coverage datasets preserve the
assumptions, partial-year flags, unknown identity rows and `not_covered` tails;
these outputs are contract-activity proxies and must not be labelled recognized
revenue or legal ownership attribution.

The transport sector also has research-only, normalized point-in-time inputs for
airline long/short work. `data/normalized/hk_transport/airline_energy_prices.parquet`
contains free EIA daily and weekly WTI, Brent and Gulf Coast kerosene-type jet
fuel benchmarks; `airline_fuel_surcharges.parquet` contains official Cathay and
mainland-China passenger surcharge schedules; and `airline_fx_rates.parquet`
contains ECB daily USD/CNY and USD/HKD reference rates. Energy benchmarks are
market references rather than company purchase prices; surcharge schedules are
regulated/issuer pass-through observations and do not equal realized fuel-cost
recovery. The files retain source URLs and retrieval timestamps. EIA retains a
source release date and the surcharge layer retains effective dates; ECB's
public endpoint does not expose a complete historical release-vintage field,
so its file must not be treated as a full vintage history.

`airline_cargo_demand_proxies.csv` is the free MOFCOM Data Center monthly
goods-trade layer used as a broad cargo-cycle proxy. It currently contains
2026-01 to 2026-06 total trade, exports, imports, trade balance and YoY rates in
USD 100 million, and preserves the raw JSON snapshot, source URL, retrieval
date and explicit `retrieved_vintage_only_latest_snapshot` status. The endpoint

## Artifact conventions

For a sector ID `<sector>`:

- English artifact: `apps/asia-markets-dashboard/.generated/<sector>-artifact.json`
- Chinese artifact: `apps/asia-markets-dashboard/.generated/<sector>-artifact-zh.json`
- English route: `/sectors/<sector>/`
- Chinese route: `/zh/sectors/<sector>/`
- Status JSON: `apps/asia-markets-dashboard/src/data/<statusFile>`

An artifact normally contains:

- `manifest.cards`: latest KPI cards;
- `manifest.charts`: chart definitions and encodings;
- `manifest.tables`: visible/detail tables;
- `snapshot.datasets`: the actual rows used by charts and tables;
- `source_health` / `source_coverage`: build-time lineage and coverage checks;
- `package_info` and `manifest.generatedAt`: snapshot identity and build time.

## Freshness semantics

- A dated time series should expose its latest observation date and age.
- `Live` means the source was successfully fetched and validated as part of
  the build, when the builder uses that status.
- `Live at build time` means the source returned rows during the build but the
  current coverage record does not yet expose a reliable source observation
  date. It is not a live browser connection.
- `Snapshot` means the data is intentionally a point-in-time view.
- `Catalog only`, `planned`, `unavailable` and `stale/unreachable` must remain
  visible; do not convert them into healthy measures merely to improve the hub
  summary.
