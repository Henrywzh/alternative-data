# Hong Kong / Mainland Airlines Data-Quality Audit

Audit date: 2026-08-07 local review; latest collector retrievals are retained
at their exact UTC timestamps. Intended use is point-in-time pre-earnings
research and long/short screening, not a claim of complete institutional
consensus history.

## Dataset grain and checks

The audit covers the normalized airline source layers, the derived
expectation bridge and the sector expectation snapshot. Checks include
candidate-key uniqueness, required-source-field completeness, date ordering,
source-page/URL coverage, currency scope, join coverage and known disclosure
boundaries.

## Current safe-use rule

The bridge and sector snapshot are safe for navigation, comparison and
hypothesis generation. Before using a number in a backtest or a final long/short
thesis, preserve its `source_quality`, `statement_period`, `announcement_date`
or `report_date`, native currency, and coverage count. Missing values are
disclosure gaps, not zeros.

The six-company RMB aggregate must not be combined arithmetically with Cathay's
HKD figures. Cathay's FY2026 full-year consensus must not be compared with its
1H2026 actual as a growth rate; the sector snapshot leaves that comparison
blank intentionally.

For the six mainland reports, the explicit operating-table anchors are the
controlling values where a PDF places narrative percentages beside the formal
table. Hainan's 1H2025 fuel data on report page 19 is an issuer-reported 31.65%
cost share and a RMB488.261m 5% price sensitivity; the relevant cost/risk
disclosures do not provide a direct RMB fuel-cost line. The layer therefore
stores a separately labelled implied fuel-cost amount from operating cost ×
reported share and does not promote it to issuer-reported fuel expense.
Southern's RMB74,570m row is passenger and related-services revenue, so its
derived RASK is a proxy with that scope rather than a pure ticket-yield measure.

## Automated safeguards

The companion test suite checks the stable rules: PIT composite-key uniqueness,
required lineage fields, official page/URL coverage, official-driver range
guards and verified 1H2025 table anchors, bridge ticker uniqueness, sector
aggregate membership, explicit profit/consensus coverage counts, the Eastern
canonical-ticker invariant, the Cathay full-year-versus-half-year comparison
guard and official CNINFO warning-PDF lineage. The current airline suite passes
136 tests. Remaining dynamic freshness checks should be run at each refresh
because EIA, ECB, market quotes and issuer release calendars have different
publication cadences.
