$$\text{Revenue}_{FY26E} = \sum_{m=\text{Jan}}^{\text{Dec}} \frac{\text{RunRate}_m}{12} = \sum_{m=\text{Jan}}^{\text{Jun}} \frac{\text{RunRate}_m}{12} + \sum_{m=\text{Jul}}^{\text{Dec}} \frac{\text{RunRate}_m}{12}$$
